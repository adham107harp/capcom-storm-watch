# Credits and data sources

## NASA data used in the game

| Source | Used for |
|---|---|
| [DONKI, Space Weather Database Of Notifications, Knowledge, Information](https://kauai.ccmc.gsfc.nasa.gov/DONKI/) (NASA CCMC) | Dates, CME speeds, particle events and storm strength for every level; the live panel through `api/donki.js` |
| [NASA Open Science Data Repository](https://osdr.nasa.gov/) | OSD-340 (simulated space radiation and blood-forming cells in mice), OSD-772 (plants grown in space) fact cards |
| [CGI Moon Kit](https://svs.gsfc.nasa.gov/4720) (NASA's Scientific Visualization Studio; LRO LROC colour, LOLA elevation) | The 3D Moon in Level 0 |
| [NASA: Artemis III landing regions update, 28 Oct 2024](https://www.nasa.gov/news-release/nasa-provides-update-on-artemis-iii-moon-landing-regions/) | The nine south-pole region names |
| NASA SDO via NASA SVS | X8.2 flare video (Level 1), X9.0 flare image (Level 3) |
| [NASA SVS: May 2024 storm](https://svs.gsfc.nasa.gov/14835/) | Aurora video and the two new radiation belts (Level 2) |
| [NASA JPL: NASA Watches Mars Light Up During Epic Solar Storm](https://www.jpl.nasa.gov/news/nasa-watches-mars-light-up-during-epic-solar-storm/) | Curiosity RAD dose, navigation-camera “snow” (PIA26302), MAVEN aurora (PIA26304), Odyssey (Level 4) |
| [Orion radiation protection](https://www.nasa.gov/missions/artemis/orion/scientists-and-engineers-evaluate-orion-radiation-protection-plan/), [NASA Science: protecting astronauts at the Moon](https://science.nasa.gov/science-research/heliophysics/how-nasa-will-protect-astronauts-from-space-radiation-at-the-moon) | Why mass (water, food, soil) makes a shelter safer |
| [NASA NTRS 20220013418](https://ntrs.nasa.gov/citations/20220013418) | Mars radio delay of up to about 22 minutes one way |
| [Artemis I, Orion near the Moon](https://www.nasa.gov/) | Title photo |

NASA images and videos are generally not copyrighted; credit lines appear next to each one in the game.
NASA does not endorse this game.
